Power-up editor: Enable/disable each of Health, Shield, Rapid Fire, Damage Boost and Speed Boost independently. Spawn weight 0-10 controls relative probability among enabled types (0 = never). Global spawn interval controls how often the game tries to spawn a power-up. Health restores a configurable percentage of max HP. Shield has configurable damage blocked percentage and duration. Rapid Fire, Damage Boost and Speed Boost have separate effect multipliers and durations. All settings save to user://boss_fight_settings_v2.json. Existing saves load with defaults for new settings.
